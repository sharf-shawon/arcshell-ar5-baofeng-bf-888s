# 📚 Resources & Links

Explore these resources to expand your knowledge and get the most out of your radios.

---

## 💻 Software & Tools
- **[CHIRP Next](https://chirpmyradio.com/projects/chirp/wiki/Download):** The essential tool for programming your radio.
- **[Miklor.com](https://www.miklor.com/):** The "Bible" for BaoFeng and Chinese radio technical information.
- **[RepeaterBook](https://www.repeaterbook.com/):** The best worldwide directory for finding local Ham radio repeaters.

---

## 🎓 Licensing & Learning
- **[HamStudy.org](https://hamstudy.org/):** Free interactive practice exams for your Technician license.
- **[Ham Radio Prep](https://hamradioprep.com/):** High-quality courses and free study guides.
- **[ARRL](https://www.arrl.org/):** The national association for Amateur Radio in the USA.

---

## 📺 Video Tutorials
- **[Ham Radio Crash Course](https://www.youtube.com/@HamRadioCrashCourse):** Excellent beginner-friendly videos on everything from unboxing to advanced antennas.
- **[NotaRubicon Productions](https://www.youtube.com/@NotaRubicon):** Specialized in CHIRP tutorials and budget radio reviews.

---

## 🤝 Community
- **[Reddit r/Baofeng](https://www.reddit.com/r/Baofeng/):** A helpful community for specific questions about these radios.
- **[Reddit r/AmateurRadio](https://www.reddit.com/r/amateurradio/):** The largest general community for radio enthusiasts.
